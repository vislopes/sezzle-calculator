\# AI Assistance



AI tools were used occasionally as a reference during development, mainly to clarify specific implementation details and troubleshoot issues.



The main areas where assistance was requested were:



\- Clarifying edge cases in percentage calculations and correcting the displayed expression.

\- Reviewing error-handling scenarios, such as division by zero and invalid square roots.

\- Getting suggestions for unit tests covering arithmetic operations and API errors.

\- Clarifying how to generate and interpret Go test coverage reports.



The implementation was tested locally, and the final behavior was verified through frontend and backend tests.
